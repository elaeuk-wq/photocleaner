PhotoCleaner Support

PhotoCleaner finds similar photos, screenshots and large videos on your iPhone, keeps the best shot of each group and tells you why the others are suggested. Everything runs on your iPhone.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue. Please don't include photos or personal information.

Why didn't my storage go down right away?

Deleted photos stay in Photos › Recently Deleted for 30 days so you can recover them. To free the space sooner, permanently delete them there.

Why does deleting free iCloud storage instead of iPhone storage?

With Optimize iPhone Storage turned on, some originals live only in iCloud. Deleting those frees iCloud storage; PhotoCleaner tells you how many of them there were.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open PhotoCleaner › Get Unlimited › Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Unlimited is a one-time purchase; nothing renews.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
