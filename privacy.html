PhotoCleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version

PhotoCleaner does not collect, sell or share any of your data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you or your photos is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your photos

With your permission, PhotoCleaner reads your photo library to find similar photos, screenshots and large videos. All analysis, including image comparison and reading text in photos, runs on this iPhone. Photos are never uploaded.

iCloud Photos

If your originals are stored in iCloud, Apple's Photos framework may download them from iCloud when you preview or compress them. That download goes between your iPhone and Apple, the same as in the Photos app; PhotoCleaner does not see or send that traffic anywhere else.

What stays on your iPhone

To make the next scan faster, PhotoCleaner keeps a cache of photo fingerprints (numbers describing how a photo looks, not the photo itself). It also remembers the photos you chose to keep in Swipe Review, how much space you have freed, and how many free deletions you have used. The free deletion count is kept in the Keychain so it survives reinstalling. Deleting the app removes everything except that count.

Deleting and compressing

Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. Compressed videos are saved as new videos in your library; the original is only deleted if you choose to.

Purchases

The Unlimited purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. PhotoCleaner only learns whether you have bought it; it never sees your payment details.

Children

PhotoCleaner collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on the PhotoCleaner website. Questions are welcome on the support page.
